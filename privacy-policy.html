Privacy Policy

LegacyRP — FiveM Server
Last Updated: September 2026

1. Who We Are

We are the operators and management team of LegacyRP. For privacy-related enquiries, contact us via our official Discord server.

2. What Data We Collect

We only collect and store data that is strictly necessary to run, secure, and moderate LegacyRP:

  • Steam / Discord / FiveM identifiers — to identify your account, manage access, and enforce bans.
  • IP address — for connection security, anti-abuse measures, and moderation.
  • In-game data — your character details, roleplay progress, inventory, and server settings.
  • Chat & activity logs — messages and session activity may be temporarily logged to enforce rules and investigate reports.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate, maintain, and improve LegacyRP and its features.
  • To enforce server rules, prevent cheating and abuse, and keep the community safe.
  • To communicate important server updates, policy changes, moderation actions, or maintenance notices.
  • We will never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR & UK Data Protection)

We process your data based on your consent and our legitimate interests in operating a safe and stable roleplay server. By joining LegacyRP you consent to this processing. You may withdraw consent by leaving the server and requesting data deletion.

5. Data Storage & Security

Data is stored securely on our controlled servers. We apply reasonable security measures to protect your information, but no online service is 100% secure. Connection and chat logs are retained only as needed and are periodically deleted.

6. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data LegacyRP holds about you.
  • Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to legal record-keeping requirements.
  • Withdraw your consent to data processing at any time.

To exercise these rights, message LegacyRP management via Discord.

7. Minors & Age Restrictions

LegacyRP is not intended for anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we hold data from someone under 13, contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

8. External Services & Links

LegacyRP may use or link to external services such as Discord, Tebex, or FiveM. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or policies of those services — please review their policies separately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated revision date. Continued use of LegacyRP after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
